About
the Group

03 / Research Directions

Research Directions

01

Multimodal Sensing

Multimodal Sensing

Integrating WiFi, millimeter-wave radar, vision, and other sources to support collaborative sensing for human localization, physiological and behavioral sensing, pose estimation, and environmental sensing.

Lead
Xuwen Zhang
Explore Direction →
02

Integrated Sensing and Communication

Integrated Sensing and Communication

Studying deep coordination between communication and sensing across spectrum, waveforms, and hardware architectures so that one signal can support environmental sensing and data transmission with greater resource efficiency.

Lead
Chuwei Jin
Explore Direction →
03

Intelligent Communication

Intelligent Communication

Combining edge computing and artificial intelligence to study intelligent methods for channel estimation, resource allocation, and network optimization, improving communication-system adaptability in complex environments and dynamic networks.

Lead
Zijian Lu
Explore Direction →

04 / Platform

Experimental Platforms

WiFi CSI · BFI · mmWave · Mobile Robotics

The platform combines WiFi CSI, beamforming feedback information, millimeter-wave radar, and mobile robotic systems to support experimental validation from physiological and behavioral sensing to dynamic multisensor fusion.
