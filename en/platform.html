Four complementary systems cover protocol-level wireless sensing, commercial WiFi feedback, 3D point-cloud sensing, and mobile-scene validation.

WiFi CSI

Fine-Grained Channel-State Acquisition

Compared with RSS-based WiFi sensing, CSI records amplitude and phase across multiple subcarriers. It provides richer dimensions and stronger spatial resolution for describing channel variations caused by respiration, heartbeat, limb motion, and other human activities.

  • Core hardware: Intel 5300 WiFi card with 802.11n MIMO and 3×3 multi-antenna transmission and reception.
  • Acquisition capability: three antennas provide multipath spatial information and improve sensitivity to weak chest micro-motion.
  • Data path: CSI data is written to SSD in real time, while RAM buffering supports long continuous acquisition sessions.

BFI

Commercial WiFi Beamforming Feedback Capture

BFI acquisition uses three device roles: a Netgear R7800 as the transmitter, a laptop as the receiving node, and a Netgear R9000 as the sniffer. The platform captures BFI packets exchanged between the router and terminal for sensing analysis.

  • Principle: the AP sends sounding frames; the node estimates CSI, uses SVD to generate BFI, and feeds it back to the AP.
  • Protocol compatibility: beamforming is native to IEEE 802.11ac/ax, so modern WiFi devices can generate BFI during normal communication.
  • Low-cost sensing: BFI does not contain upper-layer payload data and can be collected with packet-capture tools.

Millimeter-Wave Radar

3D Point-Cloud Spatial Sensing

The TI AWR1843 radar works in the 77GHz band. By analyzing the time difference and phase change between transmitted and reflected millimeter-wave signals, it estimates target range, velocity, and angle, and outputs 3D point-cloud sequences of human targets.

  • Sensitive to millimeter-scale displacement such as chest micro-motion.
  • Directly provides target position, motion direction, and posture-related spatial information.
  • Stable data quality independent of WiFi protocol, WiFi card model, illumination, smoke, and dust conditions.

Mobile Platform

TurtleBot4 Dynamic Validation System

TurtleBot4 is based on the iRobot Create3 chassis. It provides wheeled motion, anti-collision, anti-drop, anti-slip sensing, auto-docking, in-place rotation, and remote movement. The top platform leaves room for sensors and exposes USB and power interfaces.

  • Supports LiDAR-based indoor mapping and autonomous navigation validation.
  • Supports depth-camera-based human detection, posture recognition, and visual sensing tasks.
  • Combines WiFi sensing, millimeter-wave radar, and vision to validate multisensor fusion in dynamic scenes.

Applications

Supported Research Scenarios

The platform supports both low-level channel analysis and application-oriented sensing of people, environments, and mobile systems.

01

Physiological Sensing

Respiration monitoring, heart-rate/heartbeat monitoring, and chest micro-motion analysis.

02

Activity Recognition

Gesture recognition and daily activity recognition, including walking, sitting, standing, and falling.

03

People and Environment Sensing

Presence detection, indoor occupancy counting, passive localization, and LOS/NLOS channel-state recognition.

04

Mobile Fusion Validation

Joint validation of WiFi, millimeter-wave, and visual sensing on the mobile robot platform.
